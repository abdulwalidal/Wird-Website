# Wird — Sunnah Habit Tracker

> **Currently in internal testing. Coming soon to Google Play.**

Wird is a minimalist Islamic habit tracker for Android that schedules your spiritual habits around the five daily prayers and Tahajjud — not a clock. Every built-in habit comes with its hadith and source. 100% offline. No account. No ads. Ever.
