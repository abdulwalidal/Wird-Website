# Wird — Sunnah Habit Tracker

> **Your habits, anchored to prayer.**  
> Every habit has a hadith behind it.

Wird is a minimalist Islamic habit tracker for Android that schedules your spiritual habits around the five daily prayers and Tahajjud — not a clock. When prayer times shift with the seasons, your habits shift with them.

## The Idea

The Prophet ﷺ had a daily routine. He prayed Witr before sleeping — every single night. He recited Surah al-Mulk before bed. He made dhikr after every prayer. He prayed the two rak'ahs of Fajr sunnah even while travelling. These weren't suggestions. They were his life.

Wird exists to make that routine accessible to every Muslim. Every habit in the app has the hadith behind it. Every dhikr has its source. The name itself — *wird* — means a daily portion of Quran or dhikr. Something small, done every day, that brings you closer to Allah.

> *"The most beloved deeds to Allah are those done consistently, even if they are small."*  
> — Sahih al-Bukhari 6464

## What's in the App

### Free (for everyone)

**Habits**
- Up to 3 habits across all prayer windows
- Prayer-anchored scheduling — Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha, Tahajjud
- Scale Down mode — shrink goals on hard days, streak survives
- 7-day streak strip on dashboard

**Prayer Times**
- Fully offline calculation (no internet required)
- GPS or manual city selection
- All major calculation methods (MWL, ISNA, Egypt, Makkah, Karachi, etc.)
- Hanafi / Shafi madhab selection for Asr
- Prayer time notifications
- Hijri date display
- Next prayer countdown

**Dhikr**
- 50 adhkar across 5 categories: Morning, Evening, After Prayer, Before Sleep, Daily Duas
- Every entry: full Arabic text · transliteration · English meaning · hadith source · virtue
- Tasbeeh counter
- Daily Ayah strip

**Tools**
- Qibla compass — live, magnetometer-based, fully offline

**General**
- 100% offline — no internet needed for anything
- No account required
- No ads, ever
- No tracking of any kind

## Deploying the Landing Page

The entire site is `index.html` — no build step, no dependencies.

**GitHub Pages:**
1. Push `index.html` to `main`
2. Go to **Settings → Pages**
3. Source: `main` branch, `/ (root)` folder
4. Save — live in under a minute

**Locally:** just open `index.html` in any browser.
