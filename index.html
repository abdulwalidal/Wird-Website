The Idea Behind Wird

A routine the Prophet ﷺ
never abandoned.

The Prophet ﷺ had a daily routine. He recited Surah al-Mulk before bed. He made dhikr after every prayer. He prayed the two rak'ahs of Fajr sunnah even while travelling. Small acts, done every single day — this was his character.

Wird exists to make that routine accessible to every Muslim. Every habit in the app has the hadith behind it. Every dhikr has its source. The name itself — wird — means a daily portion of Quran or dhikr. Something small, done every day, that brings you closer to Allah.

"The most beloved deeds to Allah are those done consistently, even if they are small."
— Sahih al-Bukhari 6464
